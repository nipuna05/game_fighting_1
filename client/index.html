Options

You can switch while playing with P. In first-person: move the mouse to look, W/S walk forward/back, A/D strafe. Click the game once to capture the mouse; Esc releases it.

Voice is only heard by your own team (real players, not bots). The browser asks for permission to use your microphone. It needs https or localhost.

Click a key, then press the new one. Esc cancels, Backspace clears. A key can only do one thing, so it moves here if it is used elsewhere. Mouse: aim with the mouse, left click shoots, hold right click to aim down the sights (a sniper gets the scope). Keys are saved in your profile.
